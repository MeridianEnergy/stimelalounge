PRIVACY POLICY

Information about how personal information may be collected, used and protected when you use this website.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Stimela Lounge respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information that you may provide when using this website.

This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through the website may be collected, used, stored and protected.

This policy should be read together with any applicable terms and conditions relating to the use of this website.

2. Responsible Party

The website is operated for:

Stimela Lounge Glen Marais, Kempton Park, South Africa

The legal entity responsible for processing personal information and the designated Information Officer details will be added once confirmed by the business.

3. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Telephone or mobile number
  • Email address
  • Preferred booking or function date
  • Information contained in your enquiry
  • Information you voluntarily provide through forms
  • Technical information relating to your visit

You should avoid submitting sensitive personal information through a general website enquiry form unless specifically requested and appropriate security measures are in place.

4. How We May Use Your Information

Personal information provided through the website may be used for purposes including:

  • Responding to enquiries
  • Responding to booking requests
  • Discussing functions and private events
  • Providing information requested by you
  • Managing communications with customers
  • Improving the website and customer experience
  • Maintaining website security
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

5. Lawful Processing

Personal information will be processed in accordance with applicable South African privacy and data protection requirements, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), where applicable.

Depending on the circumstances, processing may be based on your consent, the performance of a requested service, legitimate interests, legal obligations or another lawful basis permitted by applicable law.

6. Website Contact Forms

If you submit information through a contact or enquiry form, the information may be used to respond to your request.

Website forms may be processed through a third-party form, email or hosting service. The specific service provider will be identified here once the final form processing system has been selected.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, analytics, security or performance.

Where analytics or other tracking technologies are implemented, additional information about those technologies may be provided through this Privacy Policy or an appropriate cookie notice.

8. Analytics

Website analytics may be used to understand how visitors interact with the website and to help improve its performance and content.

If analytics services are enabled, they may collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, device type, browser information and general website interaction data.

The specific analytics services used on the final website will be listed here once confirmed.

9. Third-Party Services

The website may contain links to third-party websites and services, including mapping services, social media platforms or other external services.

Once you leave the Stimela Lounge website, your use of those third-party services is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Stimela Lounge is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites that it does not control.

10. Information Sharing

Personal information will not knowingly be sold or rented to third parties.

Information may be shared with appropriate service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, respond to enquiries, provide requested services, maintain systems, or comply with legal requirements.

11. Information Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should therefore exercise appropriate caution when submitting personal information online.

12. Retention of Information

Personal information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Specific retention periods may depend on the nature of the enquiry, booking, business relationship or legal requirement involved.

13. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where legally applicable
  • Object to certain forms of processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Raise a complaint regarding the handling of your information

Requests may be subject to verification and applicable legal requirements.

14. Direct Marketing

Where applicable, electronic direct marketing will be conducted in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Where consent is required, you will be given an appropriate opportunity to provide or withdraw that consent.

15. Children's Information

This website is not specifically directed at children. Personal information relating to children should not be submitted through the website unless permitted by applicable law and appropriate consent has been obtained.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, technology, legal requirements or business practices.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
